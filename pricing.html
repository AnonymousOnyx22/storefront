$ about this product

onyx gives your PC a new hardware identity — the CPU, board, disk and network details that games, launchers and services use to recognise a machine. Press one button, wait about forty seconds, restart. The new identity stays until you undo it.

No Windows reinstall. Nothing is wiped. Your original values are saved before anything changes, so restore puts the machine back exactly as it was, months later if you want.

$ questions

Is onyx undetected?

Current build, yes. We test against the major anti-cheats before each release and push an update when something changes. No tool can promise forever — a spoofer is a moving target. Live status page.

Do I need to reinstall Windows?

No. That is the point. onyx changes the identifiers in place, saves your originals, and finishes with a restart.

What hardware does it change?

Machine, processor, disk, network and the peripherals the system exposes — all generated as one coherent set, so nothing looks pasted on. Full list under what it changes.

What happens after a HWID ban?

Install onyx, paste your key, press spoof, restart. The machine presents a new identity on the next launch of the game. You do not need a new Windows install or new hardware.

Can I change identity more than once?

Yes, as often as you like while your key is active. When you spoof, you can generate a fresh identity first, in about forty seconds, and it does not use a reset.

Does a game update undo it?

No. Game and anti-cheat updates do not touch it. A major Windows feature update occasionally does — run onyx again and you are back.

Can I move the key to another PC?

Yes, with a reset from your my key page. 7 days has one, 30 days has three, 90 days has six, permanent is unlimited. The 1 day key is unlimited spoofs but cannot outlive its 24 hours.

Refunds?

24 hours from purchase, provided the key has not been activated on a PC yet. Once it is bound, it is spent. Full detail on the refund page.

1. What you are buying

A licence to use the slate application on one PC, for the period of the tier you bought. The 1 day tier lasts 24 hours, the 7 day tier lasts 7 days, the 30 day tier lasts 30 days, the 90 day tier lasts 90 days, and the permanent tier does not expire. The licence is personal to the buyer and is not a sale of the software itself.

The key is delivered to the email address you enter at checkout. That email plus the key is your account. Keep both.

2. Activation and resets

Your key activates on one PC and remembers it. A reset frees the key from that PC so you can activate elsewhere. The 7 day tier includes one reset, the 30 day tier includes three, the 90 day tier includes six, and the permanent tier is unlimited. The 1 day tier allows unlimited spoofs within its 24 hours. Reinstalling on the same PC does not use a reset.

Resets are provided so you can move to a new machine. They are not provided so one key can be shared. If we see a key being passed around, we may suspend it — see section 7.

3. Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 24 hours of purchase, provided the key has not been activated on any PC. Once a key is bound to a machine it has been used, and it cannot be refunded or exchanged.

If a key was never activated and something prevented you from using it, we will refund it in full to the original payment method. Ask at the support form with your order email and key.

Nothing in this section limits any statutory right you have as a consumer that cannot be excluded by agreement.

Install it, run it, and change your PC's identity as often as you like while your licence is active. Restore your original details at any time. Use it on the PC it is activated on.

You are responsible for how you use it. Games and services set their own rules, and enforcing them is their business, not ours. Read the terms of anything you use it with.

Keys are delivered by email, usually within a minute. If yours does not arrive, ask us to resend it from the my key page before assuming something is broken.

We may release updates that change or improve how the software works. While your licence is active you get those updates at no extra cost. We may also discontinue the product; if we do, active permanent licences keep working on the machine they are bound to.

$ common questions

Where is my key?

In the email you used at checkout. It arrives within a minute and looks like SLT-4K2P-9WQ1-M7RT-8XZ3. Check your spam folder first. Still nothing after five minutes? Enter your address on the my key page and it will be sent again.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Buying a key creates it. Your email and your key are the whole account — sign in on the my key page with those two things. There is no password, so there is nothing to reset or forget.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes: two to install, one to paste the key, forty seconds to spoof, and a restart. The install guide walks through it with a checklist you can tick off.

Does onyx need to stay open?

No. Run it once, press spoof, restart. It does not sit in the background, does not start with Windows, and does not need an internet connection after activation.

Will a game update or a Windows update undo it?

Game updates and normal Windows updates do not affect it. A major Windows feature update occasionally does, and so does cloning your drive to a new disk. If that happens, open onyx and press SPOOF MY PC again — forty seconds, nothing to reinstall.

Can I change identity again later?

Yes, as often as you like. When you press SPOOF MY PC, the confirm screen offers GENERATE A DIFFERENT ONE if you want a fresh set before applying. It takes about forty seconds and does not use a reset - resets are only for moving your key to a different PC.

Can I get my original details back?

Yes. RESTORE ORIGINAL puts your machine back exactly as it was. onyx saves a copy of every original value the first time you spoof, and keeps it until you use it, even months later.

What is a reset, and do I have one?

A reset frees your key from the PC it is attached to so you can use it on a different one. 7 days includes one reset, 30 days includes three, 90 days includes six, and permanent is unlimited. The 1 day key allows unlimited spoofs but expires after 24 hours. Reinstalling on the same PC does not use one. Resets are on your my key page and cost nothing to use.

I am building a new PC. What do I do?

Use one reset to free the key from the old machine, then activate on the new one. If the old PC is gone, you can still reset — the key is tied to the machine it was activated on, not to whether that machine still exists.

Antivirus flagged it. Is something wrong?

No, and it is expected. Anything that changes hardware identity looks suspicious to antivirus. Add the install folder to your exclusions before running the installer. A new installer gets this treatment whether it is signed or not.

Can I use it on more than one PC at a time?

One key is one PC. Buying several keys on the same email puts them all on your my key page, and five or more takes 20 percent off at checkout.

What happens when my time runs out?

The app stops working, but your PC keeps the identity it already has. Nothing breaks and nothing reverts on its own. Buy again on the same email and the key switches back on.

$ refunds

What is the refund policy?

24 hours from purchase, and only if the key has not been activated on a PC yet. Once it is bound to a machine, the key is spent and cannot be refunded — that is the whole reason a key exists.

Email us within that window with your order address and key. We refund to the original payment method, usually the same day.

It did not work on my PC. Can I get my money back?

If the key was never activated, yes — same 24 hour rule. If it was activated and something is not working, email us first: nearly everything is fixable in a couple of messages, and if it genuinely cannot be made to work we will sort it out.

I bought the wrong tier.

Email us before you activate and we will swap it or refund it. After activation we can still upgrade you to a longer tier for the difference.

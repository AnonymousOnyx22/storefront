Every release, newest first, in plain words. Updates are free while your key is active — open slate and press update, or just restart the app.

1.2.0 november 9, 2026 latest

faster, and harder to undo

  • About forty seconds on a typical PC, down from a minute and a half.
  • New identity profiles for more hardware brands, so a generated machine looks like something you could have bought.
  • Restore now puts everything back in one step instead of asking you to confirm each part.
  • Fixed a case where a major Windows update could quietly reset your profile.
  • Spoof no longer stalls on machines with more than four drives.
*

1.1.1 september 18, 2026

the wash

  • Clearing old setup traces now catches leftovers Windows kept from the previous identity.
  • Restore no longer leaves a scheduled task behind on Windows 11 23H2.
*

1.1.0 june 2, 2026

disk identity

  • The drive itself now carries the new identity, not just the copy Windows keeps.
  • Drives with encryption turned on are detected and left alone rather than damaged.
  • A power cut mid-spoof now rolls back cleanly instead of leaving half a profile.
*

1.0.1 march 11, 2026

one coherent machine

  • Board, disk and network are generated together as one believable machine instead of three unrelated parts.
  • More drive manufacturers modelled, including firmware versions that match the model.
  • Support for more games and launchers out of the box — no settings to change.
*

  • Changed the computer name and the machine id. That was the whole feature list.
  • The window you see today: spoof, new identity, restore and status on one screen.
